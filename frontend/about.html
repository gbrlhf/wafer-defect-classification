The Problem & Motivation

Safeguarding yield through intelligent wafer analysis

Modern semiconductor manufacturing involves sensitive chemical deposition and etching processes. Changes in process parameters such as temperature, pressure, voltage, current, and gas flow can affect wafer quality.

WaferDefect AI helps analyze wafer process parameters using Machine Learning to identify defects and discover patterns within the data.
